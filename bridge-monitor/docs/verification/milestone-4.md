If process-tree termination cannot be confirmed, the slot remains uncertain. The Qwen adapter requests response-stream closure on cancellation, while its remote reservation stays held uncertain until separate evidence proves termination. The polling interval is configured in the project-local runtime file.

Confirmed Claude owner termination now schedules a deterministic, at-most-once paid replacement for each orphaned Qwen child. The replacement receives the original child prompt and retains the task/work-item lineage, source snapshot and GPT recipient. A queued Qwen child is cancelled and its unused reservation refunded once the owner is confirmed gone, even if paid admission is rejected by queue or budget limits. A running child becomes late-only and keeps its slot until completion or reconciliation; a late response is retained without delivery and then releases the slot. A later reconciliation can retry paid admission without re-dispatching that child. The coordinator polls an admitted replacement until it dispatches or its queue deadline expires, rather than trying once.
